Set up a sunrise

  1. In the Apple Home app, add a dimmable color light and make sure a HomePod or Apple TV is connected as your home hub.
  2. Open Sunrise Alarm, tap Connect Apple Home, and allow Home access.
  3. Tap +, choose a home and light, and set a wake time and repeat days. Your wake time is when the light reaches 90% brightness.
  4. Set the Duration and Brightness Steps. Scroll to Preview to see the progression on screen.
  5. Tap Save and wait for the app to finish creating the Apple Home automations.

Manage a plan

  • Edit a plan by tapping its row, making your changes, and tapping Save.
  • Pause or resume a plan with its switch on the main screen.
  • Delete a plan by opening it, scrolling down, and confirming Delete Plan. This removes the automations and scenes the app created for that plan.
  • Stop a running sunrise by turning the light off. The remaining steps only run while the light is on. The next scheduled sunrise can still start when the plan is enabled.

Try Demo Mode

From the main screen, open the … menu and tap Try Demo Mode. Edit Morning Light or create a plan with a sample light. Tap Exit Demo to return to your plans. Demo changes reset when you exit.

Troubleshoot Apple Home

  • If your light is missing, check that it supports dimming and color control in the Home app. Confirm that Sunrise Alarm has Home access in Settings.
  • If you cannot save, check that your home hub is connected, that your Home permissions allow automation edits, and that the chosen light has no other plan on those days.
  • If the main screen asks you to sync, use … → Sync with Home. Home Diagnostics in the same menu can help identify missing scenes or automations.
  • Before you uninstall, delete your real plans in Sunrise Alarm. For any that remain afterward, use the Apple Home app to remove the corresponding Sunrise Alarm automations and scenes.

Fix an “Insufficient privileges” error

Sunrise Alarm creates scenes and automations in Apple Home when you save a plan. You may be able to turn a light on and off even if your Apple Account lacks permission to create those automations.

  1. Check which home you selected in Sunrise Alarm.
  2. If someone else owns that home, ask them to open the Home app, tap … → Home Settings, and select your name under People. Have them turn on Add and Edit Accessories for your account.
  3. Return to Sunrise Alarm and try saving the plan again.

Still seeing the error? Try saving a time-based automation for the same light in the Apple Home app. If that works and Sunrise Alarm still shows the error, contact support and mention that you could save the automation in Apple Home.
