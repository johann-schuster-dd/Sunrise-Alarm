Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Sunrise Alarm app and this website.

Effective 18 November 2026

App data

Sunrise Alarm has no developer-operated backend, account, advertising, or analytics. The app does not send your plans or home data to the developer.

The app stores plan settings, selected home and light identifiers, light labels, and information needed to keep plans in sync in its local app storage. It also remembers whether you requested Apple Home access. Demo Mode uses simulated data in memory and clears your changes when you leave it.

Apple frameworks

HomeKit asks for your permission to access Apple Home. The app uses home, room, and compatible accessory information and automation status to show available lights and manage scenes and automations for your plans. When enabled in your setup, Apple Home syncs home data between your Apple devices using end-to-end encrypted iCloud services. Your home hub runs the automations.

SwiftUI, UIKit, Combine, and Foundation render the interface, handle app state, and store app settings and plans locally. Sunrise Alarm includes no third-party SDKs. Apple’s own platform features are governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy. See HomeKit data security for details about Apple Home processing.

Retention and deletion

Saved plans stay in local app storage until you delete them, and removing the app removes its local data from the device. Delete plans from Sunrise Alarm first to remove the scenes and automations it created. These items are part of Apple Home and can remain there after an uninstall. If needed, remove them from the Home app. You can revoke Sunrise Alarm’s Home access in your device’s privacy settings.

Website and support

This website uses no analytics, ads, embedded third-party content, or site-set cookies. GitHub Pages hosts it and logs visitor IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for its handling of website data.

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to share. The developer uses it to answer your request, keeps it for as long as needed to handle the request or meet legal obligations, and accepts deletion requests at the address below.
